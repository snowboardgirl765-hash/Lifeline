Frequently asked questions

Does Lifeline replace calling emergency services?

No. Lifeline provides guidance and quick access to the correct local emergency numbers, but it doesn't replace calling emergency services or professional help in a real emergency.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Lifeline has no server. Your contacts, safety plan, and account details stay only on your device. The SOS feature sends a message through your phone's own Messages app — Lifeline never transmits or stores this data itself.

Why does the SOS alarm open my Messages app instead of sending automatically?

This is intentional — it means you always see and control exactly what gets sent to your trusted contacts before it goes out.
